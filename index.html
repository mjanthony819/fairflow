What this is

    

      
FairFlow assigns volunteers to shifts with a min-cost max-flow solver (successive shortest paths with Johnson potentials), implemented from scratch in about 200 lines with no libraries. Everything runs locally in your browser: no account, no server, no data leaves the page.

      
The solver is optimal for the model you give it: role requirements, one seat per person per shift, personal shift limits, and three availability states. It does not model overlapping-time conflicts between shifts, travel time, seniority rules or labour law. Those are modelling choices for you, not things the solver can guess. A heuristic warning layer flags assigned pairs whose slot labels parse to overlapping times, and lists the pairs it could not parse as unchecked — it never blocks an assignment. Ties between equally good assignments are broken deterministically by input order.
